# About AI Ate my Homework

**AI Ate my Homework** is a tech blog about artificial intelligence, software, and the future — written in plain English for everyone, not just engineers.

The goal is simple: take the most important and interesting things happening in AI and technology, and explain them the way you'd tell a smart friend over coffee. No jargon. No hype. Just clear, honest stories about what's actually going on and why it matters.

## What You'll Find Here

Every post covers a real AI or tech development — new models, breakthroughs, industry shifts, and the occasional weird experiment (like an AI running a vending machine). The focus is on:

- **What happened** — the actual story, not just the headline
- **Why it matters** — real-world impact explained simply
- **What comes next** — honest takes on where things are heading

## About the Author

**Apurva Gupta** is a technology enthusiast based in Bangalore, India. With a background in software and a deep interest in how AI is reshaping work, creativity, and everyday life, Apurva started this blog to make sense of a world changing faster than most people can keep up with.

Posts on this blog are researched and written with the assistance of **Claude AI** by Anthropic — because using AI to write about AI felt like the most honest thing to do.
