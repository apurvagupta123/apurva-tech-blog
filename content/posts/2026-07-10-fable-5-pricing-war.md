## Opening Meme Hook

Imagine spending three years and $5 billion building the smartest AI on Earth, then looking at your pricing sheet and thinking: "You know what this needs? To cost MORE than rent in San Francisco per week."

That's Anthropic's Claude Fable 5 right now.

## The Story

On July 8, 2026, Anthropic flipped the switch on its pricing model for Fable 5. No more free trials. No more "included with your subscription." Now it's $10 per million input tokens and $50 per million output tokens — **the most expensive frontier model ever released to the public**.

For context: A single 3D game design prompt just cost someone $173. A normal coding session? Budget $15-50. Run this thing for a production workload? You're looking at thousands of dollars a day.

This is what economists call "a pricing strategy," and what users call "are you insane?"

## The Catch-22 That Broke the Market

Here's where it gets delicious: Dario Amodei, Anthropic's CEO, has been screaming publicly that frontier AI labs have a math problem. Training the next generation of models will cost **billions**. Running inference at scale costs **hundreds of millions a year**. To survive, Anthropic needs to generate somewhere between $800 billion and $1 trillion in annual revenue.

So Anthropic built Fable 5. The best thing in the world. And then priced it like they'd never met an enterprise customer before.

But enterprises? They've already got the receipts from the last 12 months.

The reaction has been... split.

The kicker? Microsoft isn't looking at cheaper because it wants to. Microsoft is looking at cheaper because Satya Nadella's finance team handed him a spreadsheet that said "we could save $1.2 billion a year on Copilot if we switched to Chinese alternatives" — and he stopped mid-sip of his coffee.

## The Market Just Voted

Here's the brutal truth hidden in the wreckage: Enterprises don't want the best AI. They want the **cheapest AI that works**. 

DeepSeek V3.2 isn't 0.8% as smart as GPT-5. It's 87% as smart. But it's 100x cheaper. That math wins.

Anthropic built a Porsche. The market wanted a Honda Civic.

And now Microsoft is shopping at a Chinese dealership while Anthropic's watching from the window.

## The Hot Take

Frontier AI labs have a problem that pricing alone can't solve: The world has enough smart AI now. What it needs is cheap AI. Anthropic spent billions proving they could build intelligence. But they forgot to check if anyone could afford it.
